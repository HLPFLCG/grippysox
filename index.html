Built Different

Engineered for Comfort & Safety

Every detail has been carefully considered — from the fiber blend to the grip pattern.

360° Silicone Grip

Full-coverage silicone tread on both top and bottom surfaces. ASTM F489 tested for slip resistance on wet and dry surfaces.

Bamboo-Blend Comfort

70% bamboo-viscose, 25% organic cotton, 5% elastane. Naturally antimicrobial, moisture-wicking, and impossibly soft.

Behavioral Health Safe

No drawstrings, no removable parts, no ligature risk. Designed to meet the strictest behavioral health safety standards.

Color-Coded Sizing

Industry-standard color coding for fall risk identification. Yellow, green, and red options for clinical compliance.

True-Fit Sizing

S, M, L, and XL with gentle stay-put elastic. No bunching, no slipping, no "one size fits none" frustration.

Ethically Made

Manufactured in WRAP-certified facilities with fair labor practices. Sustainable materials, responsible production.
